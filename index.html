Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

What is the minimum order quantity? +

For wholesale orders, the minimum quantity is 8 pairs per style. If you need fewer pairs, contact us on WhatsApp and we will suggest the best option.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)? +

Yes, COD is available in all locations. Contact us on WhatsApp to check availability in your area.

What if I receive a defective product? +

If there is any manufacturing defect or quality issue, we offer a full replacement or return. Simply send us photos on WhatsApp and we will help you quickly.

How long does delivery take? +

We ship across India. Most orders are delivered within 3–7 business days. Nearby cities may receive orders faster.

Do you provide different sizes? +

Yes. We offer sizes from UK 5 to UK 12. Size availability may vary depending on the style.

How can I place an order? +

Placing an order is simple. Just contact us on WhatsApp, choose your styles and quantity, and our team will guide you through the process.
